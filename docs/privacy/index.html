Summary

  • Ippo Care has no developer-operated account system and no developer-operated health database. Your profile, records, journal and conversations are stored on your iPhone.
  • Apple Health data is read only with your permission, is never used for advertising or marketing, and is never sold or rented.
  • The app contains no advertising, no third-party analytics and no tracking SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.
  • AI is optional. Content is sent to an AI provider only when you configure one with your own API key and start a task that needs it.
  • You can export or delete your data in the app at any time, and revoke permissions in iOS Settings or the Health app.

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Ippo Care (the "App") is developed and provided by Yusen Wang, an individual developer ("we", "us"). We are the party responsible for the processing described in this policy. Contact: wm88889@hotmail.com.

This policy applies to the Ippo Care app for iPhone and to these privacy, terms and support pages. It does not apply to third-party services you choose to connect, such as Apple Health data sources, AI providers or websites opened from the App; those services have their own policies.

Ippo Care helps you record, organize and understand personal health, fitness and wellbeing information. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment or prescriptions.

2. Information the App processes

Because the App works on your device, "processing" below mostly means processing on your iPhone. We, the developer, do not receive this information unless you send it to us yourself, for example in a support email.

  • Profile information you enter: height, date of birth, sex used for training heart-rate zones and the muscle-map body figure, unit and appearance preferences, goals and progress.
  • Apple Health data you authorize: for example sleep, steps, activity and energy, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, workouts, body measurements and other categories you choose. The App reads this data; it does not write to or modify Apple Health.
  • Records you create: meals, supplements, workouts, sets and weights, body measurements, notes, photos you attach and other journal entries.
  • Assistant conversations: the text you type or dictate, attachments you choose, the assistant's replies, generated cards, and, if you enable assistant memory, short facts the assistant saves to personalize later replies.
  • AI configuration: the provider, service address, model names, feature switches and API keys you enter.
  • Purchase and trial status: first-launch and latest-use times used to calculate the trial, and the product, transaction and entitlement information returned by Apple StoreKit.
  • Optional Sign in with Apple: if you use it, Apple may share a stable identifier and, depending on your choice, your name and a real or relay email address. It is used only to associate your local profile and is never required to buy or restore the Full Unlock.
  • Technical information kept on the device: app settings, caches, and local diagnostic logs used to keep the App working. These are not sent to us automatically.

3. How the information is used

  • To show your daily status, trends, charts, goals, badges and the City that grows with your real activity.
  • To save and display the records you create and to let you edit, export or delete them.
  • To carry out assistant tasks you start, such as answering a question, logging a meal or workout from your description or photo, planning, or explaining an exercise.
  • To calculate the free trial, verify the Full Unlock and restore purchases.
  • To keep the App secure and working, and to answer messages you send to us.
  • We do not use your information for advertising, profiling for marketing, data brokering or training our own AI models. We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

4. Apple Health and system permissions

The App asks for Apple Health read access for the categories it can use. You choose which categories to allow, and iOS does not tell the App which read permissions you declined; missing data simply stays missing. The App does not write to Apple Health.

Data obtained through Apple HealthKit is used only to provide the App's health and fitness features to you. It is not used for advertising or other data-mining purposes, not disclosed to third parties for those purposes, not sold, and not stored in iCloud by the App.

Health data leaves your device only if you turn on AI health analysis in Privacy and permissions and start a task that needs it; in that case only the data the task needs is sent to the AI provider you configured (see section 5).

Camera, photo library, microphone and speech recognition are requested only when you use a feature that needs them. You can change every permission at any time in iOS Settings, and Apple Health permissions in the Health app under Sharing › Apps and Services › Ippo Care. Revoking access stops future reads; it does not delete copies already saved in the App, which you can delete in the App's data controls.

5. Optional AI features and third-party recipients

The assistant only works with an AI provider you choose and an API key you supply. Ippo Care does not run its own model service, does not resell model access, and the Full Unlock does not include AI usage. The provider bills you directly under its own terms.

When you start an assistant task, the App sends the provider what that task needs: your message, relevant conversation context, attachments you selected, and, only if AI health analysis is enabled, the health records needed for the task. The provider also receives technical data needed for any internet request, such as your IP address.

Supported providers currently include OpenAI, DeepSeek, Anthropic (Claude), Alibaba Cloud Qwen, Google Gemini, xAI, OpenRouter, SiliconFlow, Moonshot Kimi, Zhipu, MiniMax, Volcengine Ark and other compatible addresses you enter. Only the provider you configure for a task receives that task's request. Different tasks (for example chat, image generation, video generation or online speech) can use different providers if you set them up that way.

If you enable web search, the search query and the addresses of pages to read are sent to the configured search service or provider. General literature searches may be sent to PubMed / NCBI. Search queries are built from general terms; the App instructs the assistant not to include your name or personal records in them.

If you choose an online voice or an image or video generation service, the text to be read aloud or the prompt and inputs for generation are sent to that service. The default read-aloud voice uses Apple's on-device system voices.

Each provider decides where it processes data, how long it keeps it, and whether it is used to train models. Some providers process data outside your country or region. Please read the privacy policy of the provider you choose. Turning off AI or deleting a provider stops future requests but does not delete copies already held by the provider; contact the provider to delete them.

API keys are stored in the iOS Keychain, are sent only to the provider they belong to for authentication, are never included in exports, and are never placed into conversation content.

6. Where data is stored and how it is protected

Profiles, records, journals, conversations and attachments are stored in the App's private storage on your iPhone. The App encrypts its local database, keeps database keys and API keys in the iOS Keychain, and relies on iOS data protection. Device backups you make with iCloud or a computer may include App data according to your backup settings and Apple's terms.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Please protect your device with a passcode, keep exported files safe, and only send data to services you trust.

We do not operate servers that receive your health records. Widgets and Live Activities display information you have chosen to show on your Home Screen or Lock Screen, where people with access to your device may see it.

7. How long data is kept

  • Profile, records, journal and conversations: until you delete them in the App or delete the App.
  • Assistant memory: until you delete the memory item, turn the feature off and clear it, or delete the App.
  • Exported files: temporary export files inside the App are removed after about ten minutes; copies you saved or shared elsewhere are under your control.
  • Trial timestamps in the Keychain: may remain after the App is deleted so that the free trial cannot be restarted; they contain no health information.
  • Purchase records: kept by Apple under Apple's policies.
  • Support emails: kept only as long as needed to handle your request and any follow-up, normally no more than two years, unless the law requires longer.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Access and export: Settings › Privacy and permissions › Export or delete data creates a copy of your records you can save or share.
  • Correction: edit your profile, records and goals directly in the App.
  • Deletion: delete individual records or all App data in the App. Deleting data in the App does not delete original data in Apple Health or copies held by AI providers.
  • Withdraw consent: turn off AI health analysis, remove AI providers and keys, or revoke system permissions at any time. Withdrawal does not affect processing that took place before.
  • Contact us: email wm88889@hotmail.com to ask a question, make a request or complain. Because we do not hold your App data on our servers, most requests are fulfilled directly in the App; we will explain how. Please do not send health records, ID documents, passwords or API keys in your first message.
  • You may also complain to the data protection authority where you live.

9. Additional information for specific regions

  • United Kingdom and similar laws: we process data to provide the App you asked for (contract), with your explicit consent for health data and for sending data to an AI provider, and for our legitimate interest in keeping the App secure. You have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
  • United States, including California and other states with privacy laws: we do not sell personal information, do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you. You may request to know, delete or correct personal information, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. Consumer health data you provide is used only to deliver the features you request.
  • Japan (APPI): health information is special care-required personal information and is acquired only with your consent. When you choose an AI provider located outside Japan, data is provided to that provider at your direction; please check the provider's policy for its country and safeguards.
  • Republic of Korea (PIPA): health information is sensitive information and is processed only with your separate consent. Data is sent abroad only to the AI provider you choose, at the time you start a task, and you can stop this by turning AI off.
  • Taiwan, Hong Kong, Macao, Singapore and Malaysia: we collect personal data only for the purposes described here, keep it only as long as needed, and handle access and correction requests sent to the contact address.
  • Canada, Australia and New Zealand: you may request access to and correction of personal information we hold about you and complain to the relevant privacy commissioner.
  • We aim to respond to verified requests within 30 days, or sooner where local law requires.

10. Children

Ippo Care is a general-audience health and fitness app and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). Minors should use it only with a parent or guardian's involvement, and AI providers may set their own minimum ages.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will take appropriate steps, including deleting it.
