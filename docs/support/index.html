Common questions

What is free, and what does the Full Unlock include?

Today and City are free forever. Every feature is free for the first 72 hours after you first open the App. After that, a one-time Full Unlock permanently unlocks Health, Goals, Trends, the assistant and the rest. It is not a subscription and the trial never charges you automatically.

How do I restore my purchase?

Make sure the device is signed in to the Apple Account that bought the Full Unlock, then open Settings › Ippo Care full version › Restore Purchases. No Ippo Care account is needed.

How do I get a refund?

Apple processes all App Store payments, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com or through your purchase history. We cannot see your payment details or issue refunds directly.

Apple Health shows no data or the wrong data

Open the Health app › Sharing › Apps and Services › Ippo Care and make sure the categories you want are allowed. Then open Ippo Care › Data sources and pull down to refresh. New Apple Watch or iPhone readings can take a few minutes to arrive in Apple Health. Ippo Care only reads Apple Health; it never changes your Health records.

Setting up the AI assistant

Open Settings › AI assistant, choose a provider, paste an API key from that provider's website and pick a model. Usage is billed by the provider, not by Ippo Care. If you see an authentication or quota error, check the key, the balance and the model name on the provider's website. The assistant reads your health records only if you turn on AI health analysis in Privacy and permissions.

Export, back up or delete your data

Your data lives on your iPhone. Use Settings › Privacy and permissions › Export or delete data to save a copy or erase it. Keep an export or an iPhone backup before changing devices, because there is no Ippo Care cloud copy.

Is Ippo Care medical advice?

No. Ippo Care supports personal wellness and fitness. It does not diagnose or treat conditions. Talk to a qualified health professional about medical questions, and call your local emergency number in an emergency.
